# About

This website helps you organize your ideas. You get your own workspace where you can put text, images and cards. You decide where everything goes. This website is for anyone who wants to organize things in a visual way. People can use it to collect notes and pictures for an assignment, or they could use it to save ideas and inspiration. It's also good for planning something or making a plan step by step.
